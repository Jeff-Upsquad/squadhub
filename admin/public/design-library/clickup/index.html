01 · Brand & Logo

Principles, assets and references

From clickup.com/brand: the identity is built on two core gradients, three typefaces, and a logo that may appear in black or white for special-use cases. The voice of the 2026 brand: "All humans, all agents."

02 · Color

A neutral canvas with electric accents

The 2026 site is overwhelmingly monochrome — near-black text on white with grey boxes — so the four neon accents (pink, purple, blue, orange) land hard. Each accent ships with a pastel "badge" tint. Underneath sits the older v3 brand palette and the classic logo colors, still alive in product UI.

04 · Typography

Three working typefaces, one for play

All four families ship as self-hosted variable fonts. Plus Jakarta Sans owns every headline (including weight 650 — they use the variable axis, not just named weights). 05 · Iconography

289 symbols, two grids, round caps

ClickUp serves two SVG sprites: icons.svg (283 symbols) and cuicons-v3.svg (287 symbols, 281 shared) — both downloaded into this kit, 289 unique icons after dedupe. Icons draw with 1.5px–2px rounded strokes on 16/20/24 grids, inherit currentColor, and switch to filled evenodd paths at small sizes.

06 · Buttons

From neon gradient to matte black

The 2026 system (CuButtonV5) is strikingly restrained: primary buttons are flat #202020, 32px tall, 8px radius, Inter 600 with ligatures off. The famous tri-color gradient survives as the AI-era CTA (CuButtonV4) on pricing and Brain pages.

V5 — current system

primary · secondary · outline · ghost/nav · tab — h32 · pad 8×12 · r8 · Inter 600 14/16 · ls −.15px · hover = bg shift .2s ease

button-large — 18px · weight 650 · ls −.36px · the gradient variant is --color-button-primary-background

V4 — gradient era (AI & pricing pages)

v4 primary gradient (hover deepens) · secondary #F8F8FC + #7612FA + 1px #DACFFF ring (hover fills purple) · tertiary black (hover 70%)

on dark "Brain" surfaces — white fill, ghost rgba(255,255,255,.07), gradient unchanged

12 · Elevation & Radius

Navy shadows, friendly corners

Shadows are tinted with navy rgb(16,30,54) at 6–10% — never pure black — so elevation feels airy. The radius scale runs 4→60px with component-level conventions: buttons 8, cards 12–16, media 14, pills 999.

13 · Spacing & Layout

14 · Motion

200ms ease, plus AI shimmer

Interaction motion is conservative — background-color .2s ease on nearly everything clickable. The personality lives in AI moments: shimmer sweeps across "Brain AI" labels and animated gradient angles rotate on agent cards (--gradient-angle custom-property animation).
